Nature statements

Trees in a forest don't just grow side by side — they communicate and support one another through an underground fungal network nicknamed the "Wood Wide Web." Through this network, older "mother trees" transfer sugar, water, and nutrients to young saplings growing in the shade.

The world ocean is home to the only scientifically proven "immortal" creature on Earth — a tiny jellyfish called Turritopsis dohrnii. When injured, aging, or starving, it can completely reset its life cycle, transforming back into a youthful polyp from which new jellyfish grow.

Deep beneath the Chihuahuan Desert in Mexico lies the Cave of the Crystals, home to some of the largest natural selenite crystals ever found, with individual beams reaching over 30 feet in length and weighing up to 55 tons.
